Process

How I usually approach a project

I like keeping the process clear and practical — understand the goal, build the structure, polish the design, then make sure the site works well across devices.

01

Understand the goal

Clarify the business, audience, website purpose, content needs, and conversion goals.

02

Plan the structure

Map out the sections, page flow, headings, CTAs, trust elements, and responsive behavior.

03

Build and polish

Develop the website in WordPress, refine spacing, check mobile layouts, and clean up styling.

04

Prepare for launch

Review responsiveness, links, forms, basic SEO fields, images, and final content formatting.
