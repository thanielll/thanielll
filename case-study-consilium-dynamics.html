The Brief

A More Premium Advisory Presence

Consilium Dynamics needed a more premium and authoritative online presence that better matched its strategic advisory positioning. The website had to communicate clarity, confidence, and executive-level trust while still guiding visitors toward meaningful enquiry actions.

The redesign needed to improve visual hierarchy, spacing, brand consistency, and page flow without making the website feel overdesigned or difficult to manage in WordPress.

Design Direction

Refined, Strategic, and Clear

I created the redesign direction in Figma, focusing on a darker premium visual style, refined typography, stronger content hierarchy, and clear section spacing.

The design approach was built around credibility and conversion: giving visitors a clear understanding of the firm’s positioning while making consultation pathways easier to follow.

WordPress Build

Elementor Pro Implementation

The final build was developed in WordPress using Elementor Pro for editable, responsive sections. The implementation focused on pixel-accurate layouts, clean structure, mobile responsiveness, SEO-ready page organization, and conversion-focused calls to action.

The WordPress build is structured for easier updates, better responsive performance, and a cleaner foundation for future content, resources, and internal page expansion.
